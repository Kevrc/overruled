# Public audit extract: Public Digital Access licences, City of Toronto

This is not the contract, which isn't public. Every figure comes from the Auditor General's report "Audit of Software Acquisition and Licence Management", December 2, 2024, Table 5 and section A.3. Amounts are in Canadian dollars.
Source: https://www.toronto.ca/legdocs/mmis/2024/au/bgrd/backgroundfile-251260.pdf

The City holds 800,000 ForgeRock Identity licences through KeyData Associates Inc.
The 700,000 licences for December 11, 2023 to December 10, 2024 cost $648,312 in subscription and support.
Management advised that 15,331 licences were in use on September 25, 2024.
The audit puts the unused cost, prorated to September 24, 2024, at $657,177.
